2. DATASET SPECIFICATIONS, SOURCE & ATTRIBUTION
================================================================================
Dataset Benchmark:
CIFAKE: Real and AI-Generated Synthetic Images (120,000 images, 32x32 pixels, RGB)

Class Distribution (Balanced 50/50):
• REAL (Label 1): 60,000 authentic images from CIFAR-10 photographic benchmark
• FAKE (Label 0): 60,000 synthetic images generated via Stable Diffusion v1.4
  Across 10 semantic classes: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck.

Dataset Splits:
• Training Set: 100,000 images (50,000 REAL / 50,000 FAKE)
• Test Set:      20,000 images (10,000 REAL / 10,000 FAKE)

Kaggle Dataset Source Link:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/birdy654/cifake-real-and-ai-generated-synthetic-images

Official Publication Citation:
Bird, J. J., & Lotfi, A. (2023). "CIFAKE: Image Classification and Explainable 
Identification of AI-Generated Synthetic Images." IEEE Access, 12, 15600-15612.
DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3340578

National Open Data & Practical DL Alignment:
Aligned with Government of India AIKosh platform (https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/)
and fast.ai Practical Deep Learning for Coders guidelines (https://course.fast.ai/).

Automated Download Script:
